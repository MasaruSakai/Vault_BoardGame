# Arkham Horror Third Edition - Official FAQ Update 9/18/20

This file is a structured digest of the official Fantasy Flight Games PDF [FAQ Update 9/18/20](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/39/90/3990f6ae-b143-43a2-a1ea-8aff693addaf/ahb_faq_9-18-20-good.pdf). The PDF contains errata, Rules Reference additions and corrections, frequently asked questions, component clarifications, and a corrected Tyrants of Ruin scenario sheet.

The page numbers below refer to the Rules Reference pages named by the FAQ, not the six PDF pages.

## Authority and reading notes

- This is an official source. Apply these entries before older printed text when they conflict.
- Text described as added or modified in the PDF is shown in red in the original. This digest records the resulting rule, not the original typography.
- Card and scenario text still applies when it is more specific than a general rule.

## Errata and Rules Reference updates

### Monster Phase and movement

- **p3, Monster Phase 202.2d:** A monster’s activation text may name a destination or prey that it moves toward, engages, or otherwise affects. It moves toward that destination or prey by the shortest possible path. See Destination 418 and Prey 466.
- **p4, Monster Phase 202.2g:** If a monster has multiple possible destinations or targets, the option closer to the monster takes precedence. If one or more options are equally distant, the players as a group choose the monster’s destination.
- **p16, Movement 455.3:** If you move into a space with a ready monster, that monster immediately engages you and your movement ends.

### Reckoning and timing

- **p4, Reckoning 204.12d:** When a reckoning mythos token is drawn, resolve every reckoning effect currently in play. Resolve them one at a time, in any order, and finish one effect before starting the next.
- **p4, Reckoning 204.12e:** If a component with a reckoning effect enters play while the investigators are resolving a reckoning token, do not resolve that new effect for the token currently being resolved. It becomes active for the next mythos token drawn from the cup.

### Archive cards

- **p7, Archive 407.5h:** When an archive card attaches to another component, usually a neighborhood deck, tuck the card under that component while leaving its effect text visible.
- **p7, Archive 407.5i:** An attached card states a mechanical effect that is triggered by interacting with the component to which it is attached.

### Attack, damage, and component actions

- **p8, Attack Action 409.5:** If multiple monsters are in your space, you can attack only one of them, and you apply only the attack modifier of the monster you are attacking.
- **p9, Component Action 414.2a:** A component action on a card in the codex can be performed by any investigator.
- **p9, Damage 416.12:** An asset cannot be voluntarily assigned damage in excess of its health.
- **p14, Horror 442.12:** An asset cannot be voluntarily assigned horror in excess of its sanity.

### Engage and engaged movement

- **p11, Engage 427.1a:** There is no functional difference between a monster engaging an investigator and an investigator engaging a monster. “When you engage this monster” and “when this monster engages you” are synonymous.
- **p11, Engaged 428.3.1:** While a monster is engaged, it cannot move unless an effect states that the investigators it is engaged with move with it.
- **p11, Engaged 428.5.1:** While you are engaged with a monster, you cannot move unless an effect states that the monsters you are engaged with move with you.

### Events, headlines, and watchers

- **p12, Event 430.9:** Event cards are used while resolving a gate burst. During that process, shuffle the event discard pile and place it on the bottom of the event deck.
- **p12, Event 430.11:** If an effect would take or discard a card from the event deck when the deck is empty, cancel the entire effect. Instead, place one doom on the scenario sheet, then shuffle the event deck to create a new event deck.
- **p13, Headlines 440.8a:** The mechanical text of a rumor headline in the codex affects all investigators.
- **p22, Watcher 495.2.1:** A monster with watcher does not prevent you from having an encounter during the Encounter Phase while you are engaged with it. This overrides Engaged 428.6.

### Traits and card-search direction

- **p21, Traits 491.3:** If an effect instructs you to reveal, spawn, or gain a card with a specific trait, reveal cards from the appropriate deck until a card with that trait is found, then put that card into play as instructed. Shuffle all other revealed cards and return them to the deck from which they came.
- **p21, Traits 491.3a:** For a deck normally drawn or revealed from the top, such as the item deck, reveal from the top and return the other revealed cards to the bottom.
- **p21, Traits 491.3b:** For a deck normally drawn or revealed from the bottom, such as the monster deck, reveal from the bottom and return the other revealed cards to the top.

### Trade Action

- **p22, Trade Action 492.4:** Assets used by an investigator during the Action Phase and then traded to another investigator during that same phase cannot be used again until the start of the next Action Phase.
- This is a timing restriction on an asset that was used and then traded. It is not a general rule that every item can be used only once per round.

## Cards and sheets

- **Archive, Card 5:** Replace the clue text with: when there are three or more clues on the scenario sheet and no markers on the board, add Card 4 to the codex.
- **Scenario Sheet, Tyrants of Ruin:** The starting space is Gilman House. The original PDF includes a corrected copy of the scenario sheet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Action

- **Can a move action be interrupted by a separate action?** No. Finish resolving one action before performing a different action.

### Assets

- **Can an Arkham Asylum encounter recover any ally in play?** No. Unless the effect specifies a location such as “an ally in any space” or “an item in your space,” it can target only assets controlled by the investigator who encountered or triggered the effect. An asset controlled by an investigator is in that investigator’s space.

### Clues

- **Where does a clue spawned by the Follow Up talent go?** Resolve it as a spawn clue mythos token: take the top card of the event deck, place a clue in the central area of the corresponding neighborhood, and shuffle that event card into the top of that neighborhood deck.

### Doom

- **What happens when multiple doom is placed at once?** Place the doom one token at a time, in an order chosen by the investigators. If a doom triggers an effect, resolve that effect before placing the remaining doom.
- Example: If a Gate Burst adds doom to multiple locations and the first placement causes an anomaly, resolve the anomaly before placing the remaining doom. If the last placement is no longer legal because of the anomaly, place that doom on the scenario sheet as instructed by the anomaly rules.

### Encounter

- **Can an encounter that allows an investigator or an ally to recover damage or sanity choose another investigator?** No. “Ally” is a card type, not a reference to another player. Players are referred to as investigators.
- **Where does an anomaly encounter card go after it resolves?** Place it on the bottom of the deck from which it was drawn, like other non-event encounters.

### Focus

- **Can a focus token reroll a die during any test?** Yes. It does not need to match the skill being tested.
- **Do focus tokens affect monster prey?** Yes. A focus token increases its corresponding skill for all purposes, including determining prey. For example, if two investigators tie for the relevant skill, focusing can make one investigator the higher-value prey.

### Headline

- **What if a headline is drawn while the investigator is defeated?** Resolve as much of the headline as possible. A defeated investigator has no investigator identity for suffering damage or horror or gaining conditions, no space for effects that spawn a monster or add doom to the space, and no skills with which to test. Skills are non-values, not zero. Assume the investigator gained no successes and apply any consequences that still have a legal target.

### Monster

- **Can players look at both sides of a monster card?** Both sides of a monster card in play are open information and may be inspected at any time unless another effect says otherwise. Monster cards still in the monster deck cannot be inspected.

### Mythos Token

- **Are the two mythos tokens drawn simultaneously?** No. Draw and resolve them one at a time. Finish the first token’s entire effect before drawing and resolving the second.

### Remnant

- **Does defeating a monster with the Shriveling spell give a remnant?** No. A remnant is gained only when the monster is defeated as part of an Attack action, not through a different effect or component action.

### Starting Card

- **Can starting assets be traded?** Yes. Starting spells, allies, and items are assets and can be exchanged during a Trade action. Starting talents and conditions cannot normally be traded.

### Test

- **Can changing a die result activate an effect that cares about the result?** Yes. Changing a die result can trigger an effect requiring a specific result, including the shotgun’s extra success for a 6 and the Cursed condition.

### Trade Action: what counts as using an item?

- An item is used when its mechanical text has affected the game state.
- Example: Sachiko Higa has been used if an investigator received her +2 Strength bonus as part of an Attack action this round.
- Example: Tattered Cloak has been used if it prevented its owner from being targeted or engaged by a monster this round.

## Specific component clarifications

### Calvin Wright

Calvin’s Friend in Need ability works like exchanging money or remnants during a Trade action. Calvin and another investigator, or an ally, may move damage and horror tokens freely between their investigator sheets or the ally card. The exchange may be one-way or go in both directions.

This movement is not “suffering” damage or horror. It cannot be prevented, assigned to assets, or exchanged with an item or talent. Friend in Need specifically permits the exchange with an investigator or ally.

### Dark Insight

Diana’s Dark Insight can modify a number of different dice equal to the doom in her space. It cannot increase any individual die by more than one.

With two doom, she can change two different dice showing 4 to 5. She cannot change a single die from 3 to 5 by applying two modifications to it.

### Gabriel and Motorcycle

When a Move action is performed, the effect of Gabriel or the Motorcycle replaces the normal effect of that Move action. It is still a Move action, so the investigator cannot use Gabriel or the Motorcycle and also perform a normal Move action on the same turn.

### Marie Lambeau

The action granted by Smoky Velvet is an additional, out-of-turn action. Marie still gets the recipient’s normal two actions on that investigator’s turn, but the once-per-round limit for a given action still applies. If the recipient already performed a Move action this round, Marie cannot grant another Move action, and the recipient cannot perform another Move action on their turn if the granted Move already occurred this round.

If Marie grants an action to an investigator who is delayed, that investigator stands instead of performing the granted action and skips that action. Because the action was skipped while recovering from delayed, the investigator may still perform that action during their own turn in the same round.

### Rex Murphy

Rex has no focus limit. He can therefore benefit fully from effects that allow him to focus a skill more than once, including Overcome All Odds.

### Wendy Adams

Shortcut does not count as a Move action. Wendy can use its additional movement in the same round in which she performs a Move action.

### Witch-Blood

“This action” refers to Witch-Blood’s component action, which says to perform an action already performed by that investigator this turn. It does not let another investigator repeat an action that Marie performed twice; it lets that investigator perform an action that they themselves performed earlier in the round.

For example, Tommy focuses and attacks. Marie wards her space, performs another Ward action through Witch-Blood, and then uses Smoky Velvet to grant Tommy Witch-Blood’s component action. Tommy may focus or attack again, as appropriate to the action he already performed.

The Witch-Blood component action cannot be granted to an investigator who has not yet activated this round, because that investigator has not yet performed an action.
