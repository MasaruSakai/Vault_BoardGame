## General: community, product, and play experience

General is a broad forum covering the future of the product, availability, storage, comparisons with other Arkham games, difficulty, player counts, scenario impressions, and fan projects. These are community viewpoints, not official statements.

### Product future and expansions

- “I want a fourth expansion SO BAD” collects hopes for another official expansion and a sense that the third edition feels unfinished because a future expansion was hinted at in an expansion manual.
- Interest in fan-made expansions, extra scenarios, investigators, apps, and Tabletop Simulator data appears alongside discussion of official support.
- Official announcements, speculation, and wishful thinking are mixed in these threads. Do not use them as evidence of a release plan or product availability.

### Storage, components, and table setup

- Organizing monsters, storing a collection with expansions, missing or lost cards, proxy images, and table dimensions recur as practical topics.
- “How to organize the monsters?” and storage threads exchange individual solutions such as dividers, deck-based storage, and expansion-specific sorting.
- Proxies and third-party accessories are practical workarounds, not official component replacements. Check rights, safety, and current availability before using linked files or products.

### Difficulty, feel, and comparisons

- Threads such as “Game too easy?” and “too few impactful actions?” contain opposing assessments of difficulty, action economy, Mythos pressure, and variance.
- Comparisons with AH2, Eldritch Horror, and Arkham Horror: The Card Game usually focus on narrative structure, board management, complexity, downtime, and replayability.
- Experiences with two, four, six, or true solo investigators vary with expansions, investigator choices, scenario, and experience. “Is it good for 6 players?” and “Good 2P Investigator combination” are useful starting points for player-count anecdotes, not guarantees.
- “How do you actually finish games without running out of time?” and “Question about length of game” discuss play-time and pacing from personal experience; they do not define a standard play time.

### Scenario structure, ranking, and spoilers

- “Official scenarios ranked” and “Shortest/quickest scenarios?” collect community opinions about difficulty, length, first-play suitability, and replayability.
- The three “On The Anatomy Of A Scenario” threads analyze linear progress, branching choices, and pushing the scenario forward before time runs out.
- Posts discussing branches or required locations are not substitutes for official scenario sheets. For a first play, pay attention to spoiler labels in the thread title and body.

### Learning friction

- “Awful rulebook” and “How to start” discuss the learning curve, initial setup, and finding the right cards or Codex entries.
- Community answers can contain mistakes or outdated assumptions. For rules questions, use the existing [BGG Rules Forum Digest](bgg-rules-forum-en.md) together with official material.

## Variants: house rules and fan-made content

Variants is the category for changes that are not part of the base rules. As requested, it is kept outside the Reference category and organized as community material.

### Recurring directions for change

- **Tempo and time reduction:** Give investigators three actions, treat encounters as actions, change Mythos draws, or restructure the Encounter and Mythos phases.
- **Difficulty easing and safety valves:** Start with both sides of a starting-card choice, add blank tokens, improve Remnants or healing, or use a Pulp/campaign-like continuity.
- **More pressure:** Add weapon reloading, extra enemy attacks, resource limits, or harsher Doom and movement rules.
- **Campaign and Legacy play:** Carry investigators, items, deaths, or progression from one scenario to the next. These are proposals for chaining scenarios, not official campaign rules.
- **Fan production:** New scenarios, investigators, expansions, Codex and encounter templates, and Tabletop Simulator data.

### Before trying an unofficial variant

- Record which rules change, for which player count and expansions, and what experience the change is intended to produce.
- Mark the change as unofficial if it is stored beside the official rules.
- Test one scenario first, and evaluate play time, downtime, board pressure, and role balance—not only win rate.
- For fan-made material, check the original thread for the current version, translation status, required cards or images, and usage conditions.
