# Pralaya Revised Edition Rules

## Setup

1. Shuffle all Relic cards, including Ocean cards, into a Relic deck.
2. Set aside as many Veda cards and Dhoni cards as there are players. Put the Dhoni with price X on top, followed by the remaining Dhoni cards in ascending price order.
3. Build the island with a 3×5 grid for 2–3 players or a 3×6 grid for 4–5 players.
4. Determine the first player and play clockwise.

Use Dhoni prices X and 7 in every game, price 4 with 3 or more players, price 2 with 4 or more players, and price 10 with 5 players.

## Turn sequence

1. Start with 3 Vitality. The first player has 1 Vitality and the second player has 2 Vitality on their first turns.
2. Spend Vitality to collect at least one face-up Relic card from the island.
3. You may purchase the top Veda or Dhoni by discarding currency cards. You may not purchase both on the same turn.
4. Refill empty island spaces from the Relic deck.

Purchasing Dhoni X requires discarding all currency cards in hand; if you have none, it is free. Purchasing Dhoni means you escape. On later turns, an escaped player discards one Relic card from the island instead of collecting a Relic.

## End and scoring

The game ends when all players escape, the island is all Ocean after refilling, or only one non-Ocean Relic remains at the start of a turn and that turn finishes the purchase step.

Players without Dhoni do not score and rank below all escaped players. Escaped players score the points on all Relic cards in hand. If tied, the player furthest from the first player in turn order wins.
