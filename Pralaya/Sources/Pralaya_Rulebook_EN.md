## Story

There was an island nation in the southern seas that enjoyed a long period of cultural prosperity. Surrounded by natural beauty and gifted with great intellect, the island's residents believed their peaceful lifestyles would go on for all time.

But one morning, a fearsome demon broke free of its ancient prison, and began to drag the island into the depths of the sea.

As the end grew ever near, the king ordered his most loyal retainers to gather as much of the nation's knowledge and as many of the island's creatures as they could, and flee to the northern lands.

## Game Overview

Collect valuable relics and escape from the island before it sinks into the sea. The player who escapes with the most relics is the winner, but be warned: wait too long to flee, and your fate will be the same as the island.

## Card Overview

Relic cards contain the following information:

- **Name**
- **Vitality**: You must spend this much Vitality to collect the card.
- **Rarity**: Tells you many copies of the card are in the game.
- **Currency**: The card's value as currency. (Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and Gold Coins only.)
- **Text**: Explains the card's effect and how many points it's worth.

*Ocean cards contain no text.*

Veda and Dhoni cards contain the following information:

- **Name**
- **Price**: You must spend this amount of money to purchase the card.
- **Text**: Explains the card's effect.

## Setting Up

1. Shuffle the Relic cards and place them face down as the Relic deck.
2. Take as many Veda cards as there are players, and place them in a pile next to the Relic deck. Do the same for the Dhoni cards. All Veda cards are the same, but each Dhoni card has a different price. The number of players determines which Dhoni cards are used in the game. Place the Dhoni card with price X on top of the pile, and arrange the rest of the Dhoni cards under it in ascending order.

   > You can check which Dhoni cards to use by looking at the back of each card. When playing with three players, for example, use the two cards with nothing written on their backs and the card that says 3 OR MORE PLAYERS. The cards with nothing written on their backs are used regardless of the number of players.

   - Use the Dhoni cards with prices X and 7 regardless of the number of players.
   - With three or more players, include the Dhoni card with price 4.
   - With four or more players, include the Dhoni card with price 2 as well.
   - With five players, also include the Dhoni card with price 10.

3. Draw Relic cards from the deck to create the island. For 2-3 player games, draw fifteen cards and place them face up in a 3x5 grid. For 4-5 player games, draw eighteen cards and place them face up in a 3x6 grid.

   **STARTING SET UP (FOR A FOUR PLAYER GAME)**

4. Use a mutually agreed method to determine who will play first. This player takes the first turn, with play passing to the left.

## Turn Overview

1. You start each turn with 3 Vitality. However, during the first round of the game, the first player only has 1 Vitality and the second player only has 2 Vitality.
2. Spend your Vitality to gather at least one face up Relic card from the island. You do not have to spend all of your Vitality; however, unspent Vitality does not carry over to your next turn. Add the Relic cards you collect to your hand.  
   (Example: On your turn, you take one Relic card that costs 1 Vitality, and one Relic card that costs 2 Vitality.)
3. After spending your Vitality, you may purchase the top Veda or Dhoni card from the respective pile by discarding Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and/or Gold Coins at least equivalent to the price displayed on that Veda or Dhoni card. Place Veda and Dhoni cards you purchase face up in front of you. You do not receive change if the Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and/or Gold Coins you discarded exceeded the price of the card you purchased. You may not purchase both cards on the same turn, and you may not purchase more than one of each card.
4. Draw Relic cards from the deck and place them face up to replace the cards you took.
5. Your turn ends after placing the new Relic cards. Play passes to the player to your left.
6. The game ends when:
   - All players escape from the island.
   - The island only contains Ocean cards after replacing Relic cards in step 4.
   - There is only one Relic card that is not an Ocean card at the beginning of your turn, in which case the game ends after you complete step 3.

## Scoring

At the end of the game, any players who did not escape from the island (players who do not have a Dhoni card in front of them) do not perform scoring; they automatically have the lowest score. The remaining players add the points on all of their Relic cards and are ranked in order of points scored. Even if a player has negative points, they rank above players who did not escape the island. If there is a tie, the player who is furthest from the first player in the turn order wins the game.

## Card List

Pralaya uses the following fourteen types of cards:

### Currency Cards

*Used to purchase Veda and Dhoni cards.*

#### Copper Coin (x4)

Spend 1 Vitality to collect this card. Each of these is worth 1 point during scoring, and until you escape from the island, it has a currency value of 1 and can be used to purchase Veda or Dhoni cards.

#### Silver Coin (x6)

Spend 2 Vitality to collect this card. Each of these is worth 3 points during scoring, and until you escape from the island, it has a currency value of 3 and can be used to purchase Veda or Dhoni cards.

#### Gold Coin (x5)

Spend 3 Vitality to collect this card. Each of these is worth 6 points during scoring, and until you escape from the island, it has a currency value of 6 and can be used to purchase Veda or Dhoni cards.

### Other Relic Cards

#### Demon Statue (x8)

Spend 1 Vitality to collect this card. Each of these is worth 2 points during scoring.

#### Ancient Manuscript (x8)

Spend 1 Vitality to collect this card. Each set of two of these are worth 5 points during scoring. You do not score points for this card if you only have one. (Ex. If you have five Ancient Manuscripts, you score 10 points.)

#### Shattered Urn (x9)

Spend 2 Vitality to collect this card. Each set of three of these are worth 20 points during scoring. You do not score points for this card if you have two or fewer. (Ex. If you have five Shattered Urns, you score 20 points.)

#### Great Statue (x8)

Spend 1 Vitality to collect this card. You score points equal to the number of Great Statues you have, squared. (Ex. If you have three Great Statues, you score 3² = 9 points.)

#### Demonic Pact (x4)

Spend 2 Vitality to collect this card. During scoring, multiply the points of all your Demon Statues by negative 3. This effect stacks, so if you have two of these, multiply by 9; if you have three, multiply by negative 27; if you have four, multiply by 81.

#### Pundit (x3)

Spend 2 Vitality to collect this card. During scoring, this card is worth a number of points equal to the number of different types of cards you have, including Veda cards, Dhoni cards, and this card.

#### Brahmin (x1)

Spend 3 Vitality to collect this card. During scoring, all Demon Statues you have are worth 0 points. If you have a Veda card, it is worth 15 points.

#### Fossil (x7)

Spend 1 Vitality to collect this card. During scoring, the player with the most Fossils scores 15 points. If there is a tie, nobody scores points for Fossil cards.

### Cards that Cannot be Collected

#### Ocean (x22)

Ocean cards have no effect and cannot be collected.

### Purchasable Cards

*Can be purchased in exchange for Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and/or Gold Coins.*  
*Veda cards and Dhoni cards are not Relic cards.*

#### Veda (x5)

Purchase by discarding Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and/or Gold Coins at least equivalent to the price displayed on the Veda card. Place the purchased Veda card face up in front of you. Until you escape from the island, you start your turn with 4 Vitality. You may not purchase more than one Veda card. Only use as many Veda cards as there are players.

#### Dhoni (x5)

Purchase by discarding Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and/or Gold Coins at least equivalent to the price displayed on the Dhoni card. To purchase the Dhoni with price X (the first Dhoni card), you must discard all Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and/or Gold Coins from your hand. If you do not have any, you may purchase the Dhoni card with price X without discarding cards. Place the purchased Dhoni card face up in front of you. For the rest of the game, instead of collecting Relic cards on your turn, choose one Relic card from the island to discard. You may not purchase more than one Dhoni card. Only use as many Dhoni cards as there are players.

## Proxy Sheet

To play this game in English, print this page and cut along the dotted lines. Place each text in a card sleeve along with its original card so that the English text covers the Japanese.

The PDF repeats each proxy text to match the number of physical cards. The unique proxy texts are organized below with their printed quantities.

### Demonic Pact (x4)

Multiply the points of your Demon Statues by negative 3.  
If you have two, multiply by 9; if you have three, multiply by negative 21; if you have four, multiply by 81.

> Note: The Proxy Sheet prints "negative 21". The Card List above prints "negative 27"; both wordings are preserved as printed in the source PDF.

### Pundit (x3)

You score points equal to the number of different types of cards you have, including Veda and Dhoni cards.

### Fossil (x7)

The player with the most Fossils scores 15 points.  
If there is a tie, nobody scores points for Fossil cards.

### Brahmin (x1)

All your Demon Statues are worth 0 points.  
Your Veda card is worth 15 points.

### Dhoni (price X) (x1)

X is the total currency value of Copper Coins, Silver Coins, and Gold Coins you have.  
You escape from the island. On your turn, instead of collecting Relic cards, choose one Relic card from the island and discard it.

### Dhoni (price 2, 4, 7, 10) (x4)

You escape from the island. On your turn, instead of collecting Relic cards, choose one Relic card from the island and discard it.

### Veda (x5)

You start your turn with 4 Vitality.
